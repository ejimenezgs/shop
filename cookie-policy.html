Cookie Policy

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device to remember information about your visit. Similar technologies, such as local storage, may also be used to preserve preferences and basic site functionality.

2. How we use cookies

Casa Glick may use first-party and third-party cookies to operate the Shop, remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the website, and show content or advertising that may be relevant to your interests.

3. Types of cookies

Strictly necessary

These technologies support essential functions such as maintaining your shopping bag, remembering cookie preferences, protecting forms, and enabling the checkout experience.

Analytics

Analytics cookies help us understand how the Shop is used, including which pages are visited, how users navigate the site, and where technical improvements may be needed.

Advertising and preferences

When enabled, these cookies may be used to build an understanding of browsing interests and to show advertising or content related to those preferences.

4. Third-party services

Some services used by the Shop may set their own cookies or use similar technologies. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling strictly necessary cookies or local storage may affect features such as the shopping bag, checkout, and saved preferences.
